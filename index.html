    The classical Tajika "point of merit" (Punya Saham). A strong placement is read as a protective, fortune-bringing influence through the year; a weak one calls for more caution.

    Your Punya Saham (Fortune Point) falls in .

    In Vedic astrology, this point marks where your accumulated merit (punya) is believed to take shape in this life — the people, opportunities and circumstances through which good fortune tends to reach you. Its sign and nakshatra colour how that fortune expresses itself. As with all fortune points, its results are not constant — they come alive when activated by favourable transits or planetary periods (dashas).
